The tech and the ceremony are not separate things here. Every session is opened with intention, because the machine is only one part of what's happening in the room. Grounded or reaching? There are no wrong answers, no "bad" readings. By the end, our hope is that you'll see what we see...something undeniably beautiful.

FAQ

Is this real? Like, scientifically? +

The bio-feedback sensors measure real physiological data from your body. The technology translates that data into color and light. Is it a perfect map of your soul? That's above our pay grade. Is it a genuinely responsive, real-time reflection of your body's energy? Absolutely.

Do I need to believe in anything for this to work? +

Nope. Skeptics welcome. The sensors don't care about your priors. Show up curious and let the data do its thing.

What do the colors mean? +

Every color in your aura carries its own frequency and meaning, from grounding reds to expansive violets. We'll walk you through yours during your reading, and you'll leave with materials to reference later.

What should I wear? +

Whatever you want. Wear the bold thing. Wear the soft thing. There are no wrong answers here.

Will my aura look different at different times? +

Yes! Your aura is alive and responsive. Stress, joy, illness, excitement. It all shows up. That's what makes the video so powerful: you can actually watch it shift.

Do you travel to events in the Bay Area? +

Yes! We're based in the San Francisco Bay Area and available for markets, festivals, pop-ups, and private events. Reach out to inquire about booking.
