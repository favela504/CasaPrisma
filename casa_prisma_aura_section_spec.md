## Notes for Developer

- The **section label** ("What is an aura reading") sits above the H2 and should be visually de-emphasized — small, uppercase, muted color. Think of it as an eyebrow label.
- The **H2 headline** is the largest text in this section. Can use a serif or display font if available in the design system.
- The **block stack** is the only non-prose element. Each of the 3 rows should be visually distinct from the paragraphs — consider a bordered container, light background, or dividing lines between rows. Labels should read as badges (short, contained, colored).
- No bullet points or lists anywhere else in this section — everything else is flowing prose.
- The typo "You're" appears in the original notes as "Your're" — confirmed correct spelling is **You're**.
