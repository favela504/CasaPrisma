The details

What to expect.

Setup

We arrive 60–90 minutes before guests and need about a 6 × 8 ft space, indoors or outdoors. We bring everything. No power is needed for events up to 4 hours; longer events need access to a standard outlet.

Pace

Readings are personal, not rushed. Each one takes a few minutes, and we read about 6–10 guests per hour. Share your headcount and schedule, and we'll design a custom experience that fits your event.

What guests take home

A printed aura photo, a digital video of their reading, and a color guide card explaining what their colors mean.

Your branding

Add your company logo or event name to prints and cards for an additional fee.

Corporate FAQ

How many guests can you read? +

About 6–10 guests per hour. Tell us your headcount and event length, and we'll create a custom experience that works for your group.

How much does it cost? +

Every corporate event gets a custom quote based on event length, guest count and branding. Travel within the Bay Area is included.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance? +

Yes. If your venue requires a certificate of insurance (COI), let us know when you inquire and we'll arrange it.

Is this scientific? +

Aura photography uses bio-feedback sensors to translate signals from your hand into color and light. We present it as a reflective, fun experience. Guests don't need to believe in anything to enjoy it.

How far in advance should we book? +

2–4 weeks is ideal, especially for November–December holiday parties. Last-minute requests are welcome when we have availability.

Do you work indoors and outdoors? +

Both. We need about a 6 × 8 ft space. Events longer than 4 hours need access to power.
