# CASA PRISMA — Brand Guidelines v1.0

## Brand Essence
Casa Prisma is a contemporary self-recognition studio.

Through aura photography and guided reflection, guests are invited to see themselves in a new light.

The experience is not about fixing, healing, transforming, or becoming someone else.
It is about being witnessed.
It is about remembering.
It is about celebration through recognition.

## Core Feelings
- Seen
- Remembered
- Celebrated
- Curious
- Connected
- Grounded

## Brand Personality
Elegant, contemplative, warm, refined, artistic, invitational, ceremonial, modern, luxurious, human.

Avoid: woo-woo aesthetics, crystal-shop branding, chakra imagery, sacred geometry, neon rainbows, festival spirituality clichés.

## Logo System

### Primary Logo
- Gold portal arch
- Gold starburst
- Thin gold beam
- Soft opal gradient inside portal
- Casa Prisma wordmark

### Secondary Logo
- Monochrome gold version
- Print materials
- Business cards
- Packaging
- Embossing
- Watermarks

## Visual Symbolism
Portal = recognition, threshold, arrival, witnessing.
Starburst = inner light, awareness, presence.
Beam = connection between seeing and being seen.

## Photography Direction
Preferred:
- Portals
- Arches
- Reflections
- Water
- Golden hour
- Fabric and movement
- Soft architecture
- Aura portraits
- Quiet luxury

Avoid:
- Crystals
- Moons
- Astrology symbols
- Mandalas
- Sacred geometry
- Generic meditation stock photography

## Design North Star
Casa Prisma should feel like standing inside a quiet portal at golden hour and being gently reminded who you already are.
